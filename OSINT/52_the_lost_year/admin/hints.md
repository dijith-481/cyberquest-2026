# The Lost Year — hints

Use these in order. Each one should move a stuck player to the next insight,
not to the answer.

1. "Elite" is not a compliment here. It is a vocabulary word from another century — the hacker's century.
2. Excel keeps a page for every year of its history, and the address of a year is simply the year itself.
3. The reserved competition is on the results page, not the pop-up list. The certificate waits at the bottom.

Hint 1 is the whole game. Hint 2 exists because iOS autocomplete eats
excelmec.org, and hint 3 is a courtesy for players who arrive at the site but
wander. Do not pre-release any hint that names the number.
