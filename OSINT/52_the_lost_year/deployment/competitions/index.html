Competitions

Every competition below is run by a student department. Win one and they will print your team name badly on a very good certificate.

The Grand Elite Tournament — by invitation only

Reserved since the first edition for those who prove themselves 1337. Entry is not bought, begged, or borrowed. The standings of the last session are public; the invite list never was.

View Results→

Cipher Joust

Two teams, one ciphertext. Break the challenge cipher before the opposing flag drops. Winners receive a trophy and the other team's leftover energy drinks.

ciphers · wit · speed

Siege Engine Showdown

Engineering against distance. Launchers are weighed before and after; any machine found shedding bolts in the canteen tent is disqualified.

siegecraft · calibration · range

Reverse Coding

Someone else's binary, one source tree at a time. Prelims in the morning, finals in the afternoon, and a whiteboard that has never been the right size.

reverse engineering · patience

Neural Net Nexus

Train something until it is briefly useful. Runs all day, online, and judges more forgiving than anyone expected.

machine learning · full day

RC Car Racing

Engineering at 40 km/h indoors. Lowest lap takes the trophy; the spare parts budget does not survive contact with the floor.

control · latency · spare parts

Code of Chivalry

A programming contest governed by honour: write, duel, defend. The judge compiles before you do, and does not forgive.

honour · rigour · shipped in bugs

All competitions conclude by the end of the day. Disputes are settled by the coordinator, then the floor marshal, then whoever finds the venue first, in ascending order of danger.
