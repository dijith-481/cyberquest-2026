Events

Between tournaments the fest keeps running: talks, exhibitions, music, and the courtyard lights that the electricians swear are temporary.

Influencers Summit

An invited speaker talks to the hall about the shape of things to come. In 1337 the speaker promised self-lighting lamps and talking machines; the crowd jeered, then built both.

Med Expo

Open Mic

When the schedule clears, the hall is handed to whoever signs up. Attendance is optional; the sound system is not, and never has been.

music · feedback · joy

Fun Zone

The closing evening of games, food and things that are better than they look. The organisers have never explained the schedule, on the grounds that it is part of the fun.

hospitality · unannounced
