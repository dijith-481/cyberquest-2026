Observances of the Realm

Between tournaments, the kingdom gathers to listen, wonder, and accidentally set the tapestries alight.

Oracle's Address

An invited speaker addresses the realm on the shape of things to come. In 1337 the oracle spoke of self-lighting lamps and talking engines; the crowd jeered, then built both.
