The Guilds

The kingdom is built and maintained by five sworn companies. Each guild opens its rolls to new members once a year, at the festival.

Guild of Artificers

Masters of engines, born and built: bronze gears beside printed circuitry. They built the trebuchets, the quills, and the kingdom's first printer — which still jams, by tradition.

electronics · machines · robotics

Guild of the Scriptorium

Letters, layouts and glowing initials. They set the chronicle of the realm in type sharp enough that the year 1337 can still, against all odds, be read.

design · letters · ornament

Order of the Cutting Room

Recorders of everything the kingdom does well and everything it does fondly. Their lantern projections are the reason most members now fear close-ups.

film · light · memory

Guild of Free Voices

Curators of the Oracle's Address and every argument the realm has ever conveniently scheduled after Vespers. They keep the microphones and the grudges.

talks · socials · volume

Worshipful Company of Scrollkeepers

Custodians of the archive and of the chronicle that begins in 1337. Ask them what the kingdom built next and they will say: see the Archives, edition by edition, as the years prophesy.

the archive · the chronicle

The King's Own Signalmen

They lit the beacons, then they learned to run the wires, and now they lie awake so the pings can sleep. First in, last out, and the ceremonial authority over every beige chassis in the realm. (membership closed since yesterday)

networks · beacons · vigilance
