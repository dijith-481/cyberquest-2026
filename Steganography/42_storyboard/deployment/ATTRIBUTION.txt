Source footage for the 42_storyboard challenge
=============================================

Work: "Budots Dance Video"
Author: Sherwin Tuna
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Budots_Dance_Video.ogg
  (originally published at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3H4A1Z64EKo)
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0

`source.mp4` is a trimmed (8.0-37.0s), downscaled (464x256) derivative of
that work, used as the luminance/hue source for the ASCII-art frames in
`storyboard.mkv`. The ASCII rendering, twin-glyph variant channel, QR
payload, terminal chrome, and lossless FFV1 encoding are original to this
challenge. No endorsement by the original author is implied.
